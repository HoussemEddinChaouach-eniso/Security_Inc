Product 01 · Argus

ArgusThe eye that never blinks.

Argus combines agentic AI with continuous intelligence so your security posture is reviewed, watched, and updated around the clock.

  • Pentest review with agentic AIAI agents review pentest findings and reports. They check the evidence, catch gaps and pre-score severity before a human signs off.
  • Dark web monitoringThreat intelligence from dark web sources: leaked credentials, mentions of your brand, and chatter from actors targeting your sector.
  • Autonomous monitoringVulnerabilities tracked from official sources (CVE, NVD, vendor advisories), matched to your assets and sent to you with context.
